# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

## Project overview

This repository contains a policy proposal for ECON1626 on the labour-market effects of artificial intelligence in Bhutan.

The proposal is addressed to the Director of the Department of Employment and Entrepreneurship, Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Employment (MoICE). It considers how implementation of Bhutan’s National AI Strategy 2025 could affect youth employment, particularly through changes to entry-level tasks in tourism, finance and public administration.

The proposal recommends an AI-Augmented Youth Pathways Package that combines AI literacy, sector-specific training linked to paid placements, employer hiring incentives and responsible AI procurement standards.
